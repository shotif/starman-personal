# Classes, powers and origins: a proposal

*Written 24 October 2026 for the owner's decisions D-33 to D-35. It fills in D-10 (three
disciplines, six classes) and D-11 (where you are from × the moment that made you) so the creator
can offer them. Decided as recommended the same day; the creator offers them
(`src/game/hero.ts`), and the powers are built with combat.*

Everything is grounded technology (D-05) and original (no names from *Mass Effect*). It draws on
the Starman canon: Reborn's combat stims, decoy flares and damage types, its places and peoples;
Control's powers, aliens and lanes.

## 1. The three disciplines (D-10)

| Discipline | What it is, in the fiction | How it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Arms** | Training, armour and the body pushed by medicine: combat stims (Reborn sells them), reflex grafts, rigid plate | Guns of every kind, melee weapons, toughness, sudden bursts of speed and force |
| **Systems** | A suit full of computing and small machines: drones, intrusion tools, quantum-linked sensors that see what a wall hides | Drones that fight and repair, hacking enemies' machines, overloading shields, seeing and marking |
| **Field** | Implanted emitters that bend space a little, the same physics as the jump drives scaled down to a body; they draw on a charge that must be rested | Lifting, pulling and slamming bodies, barriers, short hops through space; strong but limited by heat and charge |

## 2. Six classes (D-33)

The class decides which powers you can learn and which weapons and armour you are trained for.
Around the middle of the story each class chooses one of two specialisations.

| Class | Disciplines | Plays as | Starting powers | Specialisations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Breacher** | Arms | The front of the boarding party: heavy armour, every weapon, a melee blade | *Stim Burst* (the world slows for you), *Breach Charge* (a shaped charge thrown), *Brace* (armour locks, damage halved) | Juggernaut · Marksman |
| **Rigger** | Systems | Controls the fight from behind cover with machines | *Seeker Drone*, *Overload* (strips shields, shorts machines), *Spoof* (turns an enemy machine for a while) | Swarmer · Ghostwire |
| **Fieldwright** | Field | Throws the battlefield about | *Lift*, *Pull*, *Slam*; later *Sink* (a small gravity well) | Gravist · Shaper |
| **Shade** | Arms + Systems | The unseen: cloak, long rifle, sensors | *Veil* (a light-bending cloak), *Mark* (sensors find and tag), *Decoy Flare* | Hunter · Saboteur |
| **Striker** | Arms + Field | Closes the distance and hits hard | *Hop* (a short warp jump to a target), *Shove*, *Stim Burst* | Duellist · Breaker |
| **Warden** | Systems + Field | Holds the line and keeps others alive | *Barrier* (a field shell for you or a companion), *Repair Drone*, *Pull* | Bastion · Medic |

**Options:** **A. These six, as named (recommended).** B. The same six roles, other names (say
which). C. Fewer classes (three, one per discipline).

## 3. Where you are from (D-34)

Each origin is a place in the canon. It gives a starting talent, changes how people from that
place treat you, and opens a personal quest that comes back through the story (D-11).

| Origin | Where | Talent | Who treats you differently |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ringborn** | The Authority's orbital city at Earth, the Halcyon Ring: ports, permits, plenty | *Paperwork*: officials open doors | Authority officers trust you; the Wake does not |
| **Steader** | A Cooperative farm world: Stonecrop Gardens at Procyon, or Tau Ceti's fields | *Make do*: repairs cost less | Co-op settlers call you one of theirs |
| **Den-raised** | Among the Hollow Wake at Maw Roost, Wolf 1061 | *Street sense*: black markets and dens open to you | Raiders hesitate; lawful people are wary |
| **Laneborn** | Born aboard a Kettering Line hauler, never long on a planet | *Spacer*: better in zero gravity and on ships | Crews and haulers anywhere help you |
| **Beltborn** | Sol's belt crews, out in the Kuiper dark | *Hard vacuum*: suits and tools last longer | Miners and refinery hands back you |
| **Borderer** | A frontier colony beyond 27 light-years, beside the Duskfolk | *First words*: you speak some Duskfolk | The Duskfolk know your family's name |

**Options:** **A. These six (recommended).** B. Four only (Ringborn, Steader, Den-raised,
Laneborn) for less to write. C. Others (say which).

## 4. The moment that made you (D-35)

| Moment | What happened | Talent | The personal quest it opens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The survivor** | The only one who walked out when a station, ship or colony died | *Hard to kill* | Who or what killed them, and whether it is connected to the dark |
| **The hero of the line** | You held a station on a border-war front (Reborn's fronts) when it should have fallen | *Inspire*: companions steadier | Someone from that day needs you now, or blames you |
| **The hard call** | You made a choice that saved many and cost some | *Resolve*: hard choices open in dialogue | The people it cost, and what they want |
| **The one who lost someone** | Someone you could not save, and never found | *Seeker*: sensors and searches better | A trace of them, out among the dimming stars |

**Options:** **A. These four (recommended).** B. Three (drop one). C. Others.

## 5. What the creator then shows

Two more tabs after appearance: **Class** (the six, with what each plays as and its first powers,
a short clip of a power) and **Past** (origin and moment, each with a line of the story it
opens). The talents are small and visible, never a trap: no choice makes the game harder.
